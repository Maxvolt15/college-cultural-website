Members OF TIH VERBENA CULTURAL COMMITTEE 2022

VERBENA, a word of Italian origin means plants which have fine flowers of different colors. It is the name of the cultural committee of Techno India Hooghly. From 10th September 2006, we 1st started the first creative programme with the Fresher’s Welcome Ceremony. Till today we are continuing the program on Teacher's Day, Fresher’s Welcome and Annual Cultural Fest
